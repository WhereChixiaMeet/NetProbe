RouteX 3.4.9 — steady ping, no false Direct drops

- While the RouteX window or a widget is on screen, VPN and Direct are polled on one site again (the probe URL from Settings), so the ping line is steady and comparable, as before 3.4.7.
- Site rotation now works only in the background (window minimized or in the tray), together with the passive VPN check.
- msftconnecttest.com and apple.com are removed from the rotation. For some providers they answered slowly or failed, which showed up as regular ping spikes and false "connection lost" marks on Direct. The rotation is now Cloudflare trace, www.gstatic.com and www.google.com. Old default settings are migrated automatically.

Fully disconnect the VPN and close RouteX before replacing the executable. RouteX.exe and NetProbe.exe are identical.
