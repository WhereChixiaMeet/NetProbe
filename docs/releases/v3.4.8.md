RouteX 3.4.8 — live, readable monitor

- Latency chart shows the last 5 minutes by time and scrolls every second. VPN and Direct are on the same time axis (before, 120 polls meant 4 min of VPN but 20 min of Direct).
- The scale follows the bulk of the points instead of the highest spike. A spike above the scale is drawn to the top edge with an arrow, so one 5-second hiccup no longer flattens everything to the bottom.
- While the RouteX window or a widget is on screen, VPN is polled at the normal rate, so the graph is live. The passive check from 3.4.7 (no site requests while data flows through the server) only works in the background: window minimized or in the tray.

Fully disconnect the VPN and close RouteX before replacing the executable. RouteX.exe and NetProbe.exe are identical.
